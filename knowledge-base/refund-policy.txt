REFUND POLICY

Eligibility:
- Refund requests must be made within 30 days of purchase
- Items must be unused and in original condition
- Digital products are non-refundable after download

Refund Process:
1. Customer initiates refund request
2. System validates order eligibility (within 30 days, not already refunded)
3. If amount <= $50: Auto-approve refund
4. If amount > $50 and <= $200: Require single approval
5. If amount > $200: Require manager approval (escalate)

Refund Timeline:
- Credit Card: 5-7 business days
- UPI/Net Banking: 3-5 business days
- Cash on Delivery: 7-10 business days (bank transfer)

Guardrails:
- Maximum 3 refunds per customer per month
- Maximum single refund amount: $500
- Flagged accounts require manual review
